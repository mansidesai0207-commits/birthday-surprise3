Dear Ishiiiii,

You are not just my best friend; you are one of my favourite chapters of life. 🫶🏻💖

Stay happy, stay crazy, keep smiling and never change the beautiful person you are.

Once again, Happiest Birthday! May this year bring you endless happiness, success, love and unforgettable memories.

With lots of love,
from Mansiiii❤️
